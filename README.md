# Casual-Inference-and-Synthetic-Control-Engine-for-Algorithmic-Pricing-

# Overview
The Causal Inference & Synthetic Control Engine is a data science project designed to estimate the impact of algorithmic pricing changes on business outcomes such as sales and demand. The system explores causal inference techniques to distinguish the potential effects of pricing interventions from other influencing factors.

# Problem Statement
Changes in sales after a price adjustment do not necessarily mean that the price change caused those changes. Factors such as seasonality, promotions, competitor pricing and market conditions can also influence demand. This project aims to apply causal inference methods to estimate pricing intervention effects more systematically.

# Objectives
Estimate the effects of pricing interventions using causal inference.
Implement a synthetic control approach for comparing treated and control units.
Explore Double Machine Learning for treatment effect estimation.
Validate estimates using appropriate robustness and sensitivity checks.
Present analytical results through a dashboard.
